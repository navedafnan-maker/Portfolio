Socio-economic and livelihood assessment of shop operators in Kuakata

Livelihoods · Blue economy · Primary survey · n = 35 · 2026

A field study of how rent, seasonality and credit shape the earnings of general, dry fish and ethnic shops on the Kuakata coast, and how each depends on the ocean.
Context
Blue Economics, East West University. Prepared for Dr. A. K. Enamul Haque, Director General, BIDS.
Role
Group supervision, original draft of the report, visualisation, and shared data curation and formal analysis in a team of three.
Method
Face-to-face structured interviews on 27–28 March 2026 with 15 general, 12 dry fish and 8 ethnic shops, then descriptive statistics.
Policy
Low-interest credit for tourism-dependent micro-enterprises, storage for dry fish, links to certified fisheries, branding and packaging, and digital payments (only 20% of shops accept mobile banking).

Benefits to lighting: a cost-benefit analysis of solar home systems in Bangladesh

Energy & CBA · Policy review and presentation

When does a rural household gain from switching kerosene lamps for a solar home system, and when does cheap credit push it towards diesel instead?
Context
East West University. Presented to Dr. A. K. Enamul Haque in a team of three. Based on the study in Bangladesh Priorities, Vol. 1.
Method
Reviewed and presented the cost-benefit model: 20-year panel life, battery replaced every 5 years, 15% down payment with 85% micro-credit, and a $25 subsidy per unit. Three switching cases, at 3%, 5% and 10% discount rates.
Finding
Solar pays for households at any discount rate up to about 15.7% if micro-credit is available. Diesel sharing has a far higher benefit-cost ratio, so unrestricted credit can pull households towards diesel.

Security profile of Dhaka South City Corporation

Urban safety · Resident survey and police interview · n = 69 · 2025

How residents of Dhaka South experience crime, what they think causes it, and where their view matches that of the police.
Context
Urban Economics, East West University. Submitted to Dr. Biva Arani Mallik, May 2025, in a team of four.
Role
Surveyor and report co-author.
Method
Online questionnaire shared with residents across DSCC areas, with 69 responses, plus a key-informant interview with an Additional Deputy Commissioner at Dhaka Metropolitan Police Headquarters.
Finding
Residents and the police both point to unemployment as a root cause. 77.9% of respondents see street children as at risk of being drawn into crime, and low trust in the police keeps crimes unreported.

Women empowerment initiative: a framework for formal job access

Gender & jobs · Framework development

An analysis of what keeps low-income women out of formal work, turned into a structured pathway to formal jobs.
Role
Analyst and framework developer.
Method
Analysis of economic barriers for low-income women and mapping of skilling gaps.
Output
A structured framework for access to formal employment.
Link
The Kuakata survey shows the same gap in the field: 55% of women shop operators have primary education or less, and they are concentrated in the lower-income ethnic shops.
